# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The pain is real and urgent: a job seeker with no callbacks feels it every day, and the dream outcome (interviews, a job) is large.
- Buyers are easy to find: they already post asking for resume help on Reddit, LinkedIn, Facebook groups and Discord, so reach costs time, not cash.
- Delivery is cheap and fast with AI help, so a $50 budget is enough to start and a 48-hour turnaround is believable.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **It is a commodity against a free substitute.** Any buyer can paste their resume into a free AI chat tool. A "clearer, ATS-friendly rewrite" is exactly what that tool claims to do, so the only visible difference is price and convenience. The Offers lens says that is a losing position.
2. **Likelihood is near zero in the buyer's eyes.** No reviews, no before/after samples, no interview results, and the founder's own resume experience is unconfirmed. A new seller with no proof asking strangers for money scores lowest on the variable that matters most.
3. **The market's money is thin.** Entry-level and career-switching buyers are often between jobs and price-sensitive. The $1,000-in-7-days goal at $35 needs about 29 paid orders in a week from a seller with zero reputation; nothing in the brief shows that is reachable.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What result will you promise, and how will you prove it before you have customers (free samples, your own results)?
- Why would someone pay you instead of using a free AI tool themselves?
- Which single niche (e.g. career switchers into one field) can you serve better than a generalist?
- What is your guarantee, and can you afford it?
- How many buyers in that niche pay for resume help, and at what price? (open: /founder-competitors, /founder-consumer)
- What does one order cost you in hours, and what is your margin? (open: /founder-cfo)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Pick one narrow buyer and one outcome (for example, "career switchers into X: a resume plus tailored versions for 3 job posts").
2. Build a stack that free AI does not give: the tailored versions, a LinkedIn headline/summary, a cover letter template, and a short call or written review explaining the changes.
3. Get proof first: 3-5 free or discounted rewrites in exchange for testimonials and before/after samples.
4. Add a real guarantee (e.g. revise until satisfied, or refund if not satisfied), priced with /founder-cfo.
5. Treat $1,000 in 7 days as a test target, not a plan; judge the idea on whether strangers pay at all in week one.
