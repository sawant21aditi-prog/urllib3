# Monopoly lens
Score: 2/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The buyer's pain is real and recurring: job seekers need a resume that gets read, and many ask for help in public forums the founder can reach.
- Distribution has a starting point at no cost: a Fiverr gig plus direct outreach in Reddit, LinkedIn, Facebook and Discord groups where people already ask for resume help.
- Low cash risk: $50 at stake, so the lesson is cheap even if it fails.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No secret, no 10x edge.** The product is AI-assisted rewriting, the same tool the buyer can open themselves. That puts the founder in competition with the buyer's own free substitute, which is the opposite of a monopoly. The founder's own resume-writing experience is unconfirmed, so there is no proprietary know-how either.
2. **A crowded market with no small market to own first.** The target ("entry-level, early-career and career switchers in English-speaking markets") is broad, not small. On Fiverr the gig sits beside many established sellers with reviews; a new seller with zero reviews competes on price, which is where this lens says profit dies.
3. **No durability.** None of the four moats applies: no proprietary technology, no network effects, no economies of scale for a solo service, no brand. A copycat can start in month one, not month six. The $1,000-in-7-days goal pushes toward speed, not toward building any of these.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What do you know about hiring or resumes that most sellers do not? (Have you hired, recruited or written resumes before?)
- Which narrow niche could you be the obvious best choice for, e.g. one industry, one role or one type of career switch, where you have inside knowledge?
- Why would a buyer pay you rather than paste their resume into a free AI tool?
- How do the first 100 customers arrive, and what does each cost in time? A new Fiverr seller with no reviews: how many orders in week one is realistic?
- Open questions I cannot answer without inventing numbers: how many sellers offer this and at what prices (/founder-competitors); whether this buyer will pay $25-$60 over a free AI tool (/founder-consumer); whether $35 per order at the hours each order takes can reach $1,000 in 7 days (/founder-cfo).

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable (PASS). What would reopen it: a narrow niche tied to real inside knowledge of how one field hires (a secret), with evidence from /founder-competitors that no seller owns that niche, and from /founder-consumer that its buyers prefer a specialist over free AI.
