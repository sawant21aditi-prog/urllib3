## 0:39 · Ch.1 · The Rope

15. While the rope is taut, its tension holds those pads back. [F3]
16. But if the rope breaks, the tension vanishes, the spring fires, and the pads bite into the teeth. [F3]
17. Think about that. The brake isn't something anyone switches on. It's what happens when everything else fails. [F4]
