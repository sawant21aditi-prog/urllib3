# Page Strategy: faceless, code-animated explainer Reels

Prepared 2026-10-04 by the virality agent. **Evidence** means a cited platform statement or a published dataset. **Estimate** means my judgement. No plan can guarantee virality. Short-form reach is heavy-tailed: most Reels get a few hundred views and a few get almost all of the reach. This plan aims to win more of those draws and to compound the wins.

Shared traits of the top videos (my synthesis of the sources above, with evidence for the general pattern):
- **Topics:** everyday objects with a hidden reason, "it was designed that way on purpose", disasters that changed design, and psychology "tricks" played on you.
- **Hook phrasing:** second person plus accusation or reversal ("This button is lying to you", "You've been doing X wrong"). The claim lands within 1–2 seconds, and nothing is set up before it.
- **Length:** 25–50s for fact explainers. Reels can now be 3 minutes long ([Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-three-minute-reels)), but 30–60s remains the sweet spot for one-fact explainers (estimate). TikTok pays only for videos of 60s or more (see section 10).
- **Style:** constant motion, a visual change every 1–3s, burned-in captions, an end that loops into the start, and no end cards.

### 1b. Topic clusters going viral now (evidence plus estimate)
1. **Hidden or placebo design:** fake buttons, dummy controls, "it's on purpose". This is our launch cluster.
2. **Aviation oddities:** windows, black boxes, cabin lighting, ashtrays. These come with very high natural curiosity.
3. **Food-brand secrets:** the McDonald's ice cream machine, Heinz 57, Pringles. They drive sends because they are about shared brands.
4. **Psychology of everyday life:** pricing, waiting, the perception of time. Psychology and human behaviour is a named trending edu niche ([flowshorts](https://flowshorts.app/tiktok-ideas/education)).
5. **"History of an ordinary thing":** time zones, colours, icons.
6. **Tech myths:** closing apps, airplane mode, chargers.

### 1c. Instagram growth mechanics (current)
- **Top ranking signals (evidence).** Adam Mosseri (Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** as the most important signals. Sends matter most for reach to **non-followers**, and likes matter most for followers ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/), [dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)). **Implication:** every Reel needs a built-in reason to DM it to someone.
- **Skip rate and retention chart (evidence).** Since Aug 2025, Reels Insights show a **skip rate**, which is the share of viewers who leave within 3s, and a second-by-second retention curve ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)). Industry heuristics put a skip rate under about 30–40% as healthy and over 50% as a broken hook. These are heuristics, not official benchmarks.
- **Trial Reels (evidence, with a caveat).** A Trial Reel is shown to non-followers first. Its metrics appear after about 24h, and you can turn on auto-share to followers if it performs within 72h ([Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-trial-reels-work-72-hours/), [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132)). **Eligibility is reported inconsistently.** Mosseri's wider rollout cited **public professional accounts with 1,000+ followers**, while other sources say it is open to all professional accounts. **Assume a brand-new page may not see the Trial toggle until it reaches about 1k followers.** Check the share screen.
- **Originality (evidence).** Accounts that mostly post content they did not create, or did not meaningfully transform, lose eligibility for recommendations to non-followers. This is judged over a rolling 30-day window. When content is duplicated, Instagram recommends only the original ([PetaPixel, Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/), [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy)). Our code-generated, original-script Reels are favoured here. **Never** post another platform's watermarked export: watermarked Reels are reported to lose most of their reach ([posteverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-to-remove-tiktok-watermark-before-posting)).
- **AI rules (evidence).** On 31 Aug 2026, Instagram renamed its label to "AI-generated profile". Its reach limits apply to profiles that **feature an AI-generated person** and are unlabelled. AI used for graphics, edits or captions does not need that label ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/instagram-renames-ai-creator-label-and-restricts-unlabeled-ai-generated-profiles/), [implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/instagram-will-cut-the-reach-of-ai-personas-that-skip-its-new-label/)). We have no AI persona, so the label does not apply. Our voice is TTS. **Recommendation:** never present the voice as a real human host, and if Instagram's AI-content prompt appears at upload, answer it honestly. Add "Animated & narrated in code" to the bio to be transparent.
- **Hashtags (evidence).** Instagram caps posts at **5 hashtags**, and Mosseri says hashtags help search but "don't increase your reach" ([inmagic](https://inmagic.ai/how-many-hashtags-on-instagram/), [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/instagram-limite-hashtags-5-par-publication/)). Keyword-rich captions matter more.
- **Profile SEO (evidence).** Google has indexed public professional profiles and Reels since July 2025. The **Name field** is the most heavily weighted searchable field, followed by the handle, bio and captions ([inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-seo), [Search Influence](https://www.searchinfluence.com/blog/instagram-seo-playbook-how-to-win-over-google-and-ai-search/)).
- **Reposts (evidence).** A native Repost button arrived in Aug 2025 ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/repost-on-instagram/)). Reposts by viewers are another distribution path, so we should ask for them in captions occasionally.

### 1d. The gap we can own
- **Format gap:** Most "facts" pages use stock footage, AI slop or 3D (Zack D clones). Very few post a **Vox-grade editorial collage daily**, and we can because our marginal cost is $0. The cream, charcoal and yellow look is recognisable in one frame of the feed. That is a brand asset, and Instagram's originality push rewards it.
- **Topic gap:** We will own **"things that are secretly designed to manipulate or protect you"**: placebo buttons, hidden safety features and design decisions. That is narrower than "random facts", which makes it easier for the algorithm to classify us and for people to follow. It also has a near-unlimited backlog.
- **Trust gap:** Fact pages are full of myths. A visible **"Sources in comments"** habit plus honest hedges ("often", "in many US elevators") is a differentiator, and it protects the page from "well actually" pile-ons that hurt trust.

## 4. Profile picture (exactly one spec, rendered in code)

**Concept: "the paper question mark with a button for a dot."**
- Canvas 1080×1080 px, exported as PNG. Instagram crops it to a circle that shows at about 110px on the profile and about 32px in the feed.
- **Background:** solid accent yellow `#F5C518` filling the whole canvas, so the circle crop is a solid yellow disc. Add a subtle halftone of charcoal dots at 6% opacity, 9px pitch, concentrated toward the bottom-right as a "shadow".
- **Main shape:** one bold **charcoal `#1E1E1E` question mark** (use the reel's heavy display font at about 900 weight). It is about 640px tall and centred optically, with its centre about 20px above the canvas centre. It is rotated −6°, with a **24px white sticker border** around the glyph and a hard drop shadow (charcoal at 25%, offset 10px/12px). That makes it read as a paper cutout.
- **The dot:** the full stop of the question mark is replaced by a **round elevator-style button**: a cream `#F3EBDD` disc 150px across with a 14px charcoal ring and a small yellow inner glow. It is our signature element, and it ties to Reel #1 and the "hidden mechanism" idea.
- **Washi tape:** one strip of cream tape, 260×70px at 35% transparency with a torn-edge texture. It sits at −30° across the top-left of the question mark's curve. Keep it inside the inscribed circle, within a radius of about 470px of the centre, so the crop does not clip it.
- **No text and no letters except the glyph.**
- **Why it reads at 110px:** the avatar is two shapes, one dark and one bright, with about 11:1 contrast between charcoal and yellow. The question mark is a universally recognised silhouette that means "explained". The solid yellow disc stands out against Instagram's white and black UI and matches the yellow accent in every Reel, so viewers connect the avatar to the videos. Fine detail such as tape and halftone only shows at larger sizes, as a reward for people who look closer.

**Content pillars (4):**

The format is: object accused (hook), who built it fake and why, the one exception (open loop), and the payoff. Series numbering drives profile binge-watching and follows ("I want the next one").

## 8. Volume and timing

**Evidence:** Buffer's analysis of about 2M posts from 100k accounts found that reach per post rises with frequency. Going from 1–2 to 3–5 posts a week gives the biggest lift (about +12%), and 6–9 and then 10+ posts a week still add gains, with diminishing returns ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/)). Posting more often does not reduce reach per post. **Estimate:** for a new account, each Reel is a lottery ticket into non-follower distribution, so volume matters more than it does for an established page.

| Phase | Reels | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Month 1** | **1 per day (7/week)**, and only those that pass the QA gate. If one fails, skip the day rather than post a weak Reel. | It maximises at-bats and gives the algorithm data to classify the page. Our cost per Reel is $0. |
| **Months 2–3** | 1 main Reel per day, plus **1–2 Trial Reel hook variants per week** once Trial Reels are available. Carry winners forward. | Variants are re-cuts with a different hook line, cover or first 3s, so production cost stays near zero. |
| **Months 4+** | Keep 7 a week. Go to 10–14 a week only if the QA pass rate is at least 90% and the median reach of the last 14 posts is not falling. Add carousels such as "the 5 sources behind this Reel" 2× a week for saves. | Diminishing returns past about 10 a week. Quality dilution is the bigger risk. |

**Best posting times.** Evidence: Sprout Social's 2026 analysis of about 2B engagements found the strongest Reels windows were Tue 1–7pm, Wed 12–9pm and Thu 12–2pm, all local time, and noted that audience activity matters more than format ([fliki summary of Sprout data](https://fliki.ai/blog/best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram)). Later's 6M-post study found an early-morning peak ([Later](https://later.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram/)). Studies disagree, so:
- **Default:** post at **12:00–1:00 pm US Eastern** on weekdays and **10:00 am ET** on weekends. Our topics are US-leaning, and US audiences monetise best.
- **Test in weeks 2–3:** alternate 12pm and 7pm ET. Once there are 14 or more posts, switch to the "Most active times" in Insights.

**Trial Reels testing protocol** (once the toggle is available; it may need about 1k followers):
1. Post the main version normally at the scheduled time.
2. Within 24h, post 1–2 **Trial** variants of the same Reel that differ in **only one** of: the hook line (frames 0–3s), the cover text, or the length (−20%).
3. At 24h, compare views, skip rate, shares and average watch time against the main version.
4. If a variant beats the main version by 25% or more on views **and** has a lower skip rate, share it to followers (or let auto-share do it) and record the winning hook template in `reel_formula.json` (`hook_wins`).
5. Never trial-test more than one variable at a time. Keep a log with date, topic, variable, metric deltas and verdict.

**Before the Trial toggle exists:** use the next day's Reel as the test. Rotate hook templates 1–10 and track skip rate per template.

**First-hour engagement routine** (about 20 minutes a day; manual):
- T−0: post, then immediately add the **pinned comment** (the question plus sources).
- T+0–5 min: share the Reel to your Story with a poll sticker ("Did you know this? Yes/No"). Polls drive taps, and taps send profile signals.
- T+0–60 min: reply to **every** comment with a question or an extra fact, so the comment count roughly doubles. Pin the best viewer comment if it's funny.
- T+15 min: send the Reel to 3–5 friends or group chats who genuinely find it interesting. Real sends from real people help; never use engagement pods or bought engagement.
- T+60 min: check skip rate and early shares. If the skip rate is over 50%, note the hook as a loser.
- Never delete and repost a Reel. Never buy followers or views: it poisons audience signals and risks account integrity.

**Collab and remix tactics:**
- **Collab posts** (co-authored Reels that appear on both profiles): from week 3, offer free custom explainers to 5–10 related accounts in the 10k–200k range (design, aviation, history and science pages), with their topic in our style. Collab Reels inherit the partner's audience. This is the single fastest follower lever for a small page (estimate).
- **Reply-to-comment Reels:** turn a good viewer question into the next Reel, starting with "@user asked...". This raises comment intent.
- **Respond to trends with facts:** when a news story touches a backlog topic (a plane incident, a product recall), move that topic up. Keep it respectful, with no tragedy-baiting.
- **Remix:** keep remixing enabled. Duetting or remixing bigger accounts' myths with "Actually..." corrections is risky. Only do it with a source on screen and a kind tone.

**Cross-posting: YES to both.**
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload the same clean file (no watermark) with a title-first caption that states the question in the first line, e.g. "Why the elevator close button does nothing #shorts". Shorts gives a second search-driven surface plus long-tail views ([posteverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-to-cross-post-reels-shorts-and-tiktok-at-the-same-time)).
- **TikTok:** upload the clean file. **Also render a 61–75s "extended cut"**, adding one extra context beat plus the exception or debate, because Creator Rewards pays only for videos of 1 minute or more ([ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/guides/creator-rewards-program/)).
- There is no evidence of a cross-platform duplicate penalty. Watermarks and recycled platform audio are what hurt ([socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/avoid-content-duplication-penalties-cross-posting-2026)). Write captions per platform.

## 9. Growth targets (estimates, with wide variance)

Honest note: there is no public dataset for "new faceless edu pages posting daily". These ranges are my estimates, based on how short-form distribution behaves (heavy-tailed: one Reel can bring 80% of a month's followers). The **median** new page does much worse than the top of these ranges. A single 1M-view Reel can jump the page a whole row. Treat the **low** column as "the format is working but has not hit" and the **high** column as "one or more breakouts".

| Horizon | Followers: low / likely / high | Monthly views (all Reels): low / likely / high |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | 100 / 500–2,000 / 20k+ | 10k / 50k–300k / 3M+ |
| Month 3 | 1k / 3k–15k / 75k+ | 100k / 0.5M–3M / 15M+ |
| Month 6 | 3k / 10k–50k / 250k+ | 300k / 1M–8M / 40M+ |
| Month 12 | 5k / 25k–150k / 1M+ | 0.5M / 2M–20M / 100M+ |

**KPIs that predict a breakout** (benchmarks are industry heuristics or my estimates, not Meta-published):

| KPI | Where to find it | Healthy | Breakout signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skip rate (inverse of 3s hold) | Reel Insights → Skip rate | under 40% | under 25% |
| Average watch time / length | Insights → Avg. watch time | 60%+ | 85%+ (rewatches push it over 100%) |
| Sends per reach | Shares ÷ Accounts reached | 1%+ | 3%+ |
| Saves per reach | Saves ÷ Accounts reached | 0.5%+ | 2%+ |
| Follows per 1k reach | Insights → Follows | 2+ | 8+ |
| Non-follower reach % | Insights → Reach breakdown | 70%+ | 90%+ |

**Kill and double-down rules:**
- **Double down:** if a Reel beats the 30-day median reach by 3× or more **or** has sends per reach of 3% or more, make 2–3 follow-ups on the **same topic cluster** within 7 days (a sequel, "part 2", the same hook template) and pin it.
- **Kill a hook template:** retire it after 3 Reels with a skip rate over 50%.
- **Kill a pillar:** demote it after 6 posts at under 0.5× the page median reach. Keep one test post per month in case the audience shifts.
- **Kill a format element:** if retention curves show the same drop at the same device (such as the history pivot at about 5s) on 3 or more Reels, change the formula in `reel_formula.json`.
- **Page-level pivot check at day 60:** if follows per 1k reach stay under 1 and median reach is under 1k, re-test the niche framing, not just the topics: for example "aviation secrets" only, or "brand secrets" only. Do not abandon the style.

## 11. Honest expectations for month 1

- **Most likely:** 30 Reels, a few hundred to a few thousand followers, and typical Reels at 300–5,000 views. Instagram tests new accounts with small batches of non-followers, and early numbers mostly measure hook strength, not quality.
- **Likely at least once:** a "small spike" Reel at 10–50k views. When that happens, it is the signal to double down on its topic cluster.
- **Possible but not plannable:** one 500k–2M+ breakout. Reel #1 and the crosswalk Reel are our best bets (high sends, universal objects). If it happens, have the next 5 Placebo Files ready and pinned.
- **Revenue:** expect close to $0 from platforms in month 1. The only realistic month-1 money is from services, templates and affiliate links.
- **What would worry me:** a skip rate consistently over 50% (fix the hooks), or "this is wrong" comments (fix the hedging, because trust compounds). Low views alone in weeks 1–2 are **not** a reason to change the style.
- **Your daily cost:** about 20–30 minutes (post, pinned comment, Story share, reply to comments). Skipping the first-hour routine is the biggest avoidable loss.
