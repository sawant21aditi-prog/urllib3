# Reel #2: Why Pen Caps Have a Hole (publishing pack)

## Caption
Why do pen caps have a hole in the top? 🖊️

On many pens, like the classic BIC, that hole is a safety vent. It's designed to let air through if a cap is accidentally inhaled, under the international standard ISO 11540 (at least 8 litres of air per minute). The hole that helps the ink flow is a different one, on the side of the barrel.

Safety: if someone is choking or can't breathe, call emergency services (911 / 999 / 112) right away.
Sources: BIC; ISO 11540:2021; BS 7272:1990; Snopes fact-check; Int J Pediatr Otorhinolaryngol (2012). Details in comments.
